# Demo image sources

The demo photos are local copies of Wikimedia Commons images searched on 7 September 2026. They are illustrative evidence for the POC and are not photographs from the stated Selangor locations, except where an entry explicitly names a Selangor news source below.

- Construction debris: [Building construction-demolition-repair waste](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Building_construction-demolition-repair_waste_being_dumped_in_streets_in_Bengaluru.jpg), [Fly-tipping construction waste](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fly-tipping_construction_waste_in_Parco_Alto_Milanese_-_Busto_Arsizio,_Lombardy,_Italy.jpg), and [Illegal dumping at site of future condominiums](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Illegal_Dumping_at_site_of_future_condominiums_(49946254348).jpg)

- Household waste: [Wheelie bin enclosure dumped rubbish bags](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wheelie_bin_enclosure_dumped_rubbish_bags_Lordship_Lane_Tottenham,_London,_England.jpg)

Department logos are downloaded from the official [MBSA](https://www.mbsa.gov.my/ms-my/Halaman/homepage.aspx), [MBPJ](https://www.mbpj.gov.my/ms/mbpj/profil/logo), [MBSJ](https://www.mbsj.gov.my/en/node/131), and [MBDK](https://www.mbdk.gov.my/ms/muat-turun) websites and stored under `/public/brand/departments`.

The landing-page gallery uses Selangor-specific reporting coverage from [The Star's Dengkil report](https://www.thestar.com.my/metro/metro-news/2023/05/24/illegal-dumping-open-burning-hotspot-in-dengkil), [Puchong report](https://www.thestar.com.my/metro/metro-news/2023/10/24/mbsj-continues-crackdown-on-privately-owned-land-turned-into-illegal-dump-site), [Kapar road report](https://www.thestar.com.my/metro/metro-news/2026/06/12/council-to-fix-roads-after-kapar-factory-owners-mount-complaints), and [Subang Jaya road report](https://www.thestar.com.my/metro/metro-news/2026/01/24/patchwork-repairs-fail-subang-jaya-motorists).

The additional illustrative cases use the gallery evidence for Dengkil, Puchong, Kapar and Subang Jaya. Their location context is documented by [Bernama's Kapar report](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2188258), [Sinar Harian's Dengkil enforcement report](https://www.sinarharian.com.my/article/736018/edisi/selangor-kl/empat-tapak-sampah-haram-dikenal-pasti-di-dengkil), [MPHS's Serendah hotspot cleanup](https://mphs.gov.my/en/mphs/media/archives/news-archives/illegal-garbage-hot-spots-are-cleared-by-mphs), and the [Selangor government clipping on the Klang pothole at Jalan Raja Nong / Jalan Seruling 59](https://www.selangor.gov.my/selangor/resources/user_102/Keratan_Akhbar_30_Jan_20191.pdf). The POC does not represent these news photographs as new, live reports.
